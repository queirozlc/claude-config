# Live product advice

Use for explicit evaluation of a feature, price, conversion flow, or growth channel. Do not use as an implementation gate.

Read relevant existing growth state or authorized analytics. Ask for metrics that affect the requested decision. Conversion, activation, paid conversion, churn, and active-user counts can help, but all six are not prerequisites. Define the event, time window, and cohort before comparing rates.

Find the likely constraint and explain how the proposed change could affect it. Missing analytics lowers confidence; propose measurement without replacing the user's question. Use [growth engines](../references/lean-startup.md) for the controlling metric and [strategy](../references/good-strategy-bad-strategy.md) for competing priorities.

Trace demand to evidence. Repeated past user behavior or a costly commitment is stronger than a competitor example or stated intent. Use [customer interviews](../references/mom-test.md) to understand motivation. Do not treat one metric as the sole reason a usability, accessibility, reliability, or contractual change can be worthwhile.

Select references by question:
- [Positioning](../references/obviously-awesome.md): segment, alternatives, value, and market category.
- [Experiments](../references/testing-business-ideas.md): a test with a clear hypothesis and observable outcome.
- [Usability](../references/dont-make-me-think.md): clarity and interaction problems.
- [Persuasion](../references/psychology-of-persuasion.md): truthful offers and evidence. Do not invent scarcity, quotes, or results.

For pricing, distinguish value uncertainty from price uncertainty and compare actual alternatives. For channel advice, consider retention and unit economics before recommending more acquisition. Treat numerical examples in references as context-dependent, not automatic targets.

Finish with the recommendation, supporting evidence, important uncertainty, and a test that could change the decision. Do not require an override keyword to proceed. Update growth-state only when requested or required by the established project workflow.
